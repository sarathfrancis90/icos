APP TITLE (30 char max):
Icos - Daily Path Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 char max):
One line. Every cell. A daily path puzzle with exactly one solution.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char max):
Icos is a daily path puzzle. Draw one continuous line through every cell of the grid, visiting the numbered waypoints in order, without lifting your finger. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

THE ORIGINAL PATH PUZZLE
In 1857 the mathematician William Rowan Hamilton invented the Icosian game: find a route that visits every point exactly once. Icos brings that idea to your phone with a fresh grid every day.

HOW TO PLAY
Start at waypoint 1 and trace your line through the grid. Visit each numbered waypoint in sequence. Fill every cell, leave no gaps, and never cross your own path. Simple rules, deep strategy.

ONE PUZZLE A DAY, FOR EVERYONE
Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle each day. Monday starts gentle with a 5x5 grid. By Sunday you are facing an 8x8. Build a streak, earn a weekly streak freeze, and compare your time with friends.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Create a group, share a six-character invite code or a QR code, and race on daily and weekly leaderboards. A live activity feed shows you when your friends solve.

PRACTICE AND ARCHIVE
Unlimited practice puzzles generated right on your device, plus the last 30 days of daily puzzles to catch up on.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
Colorblind palettes with pattern overlays. Full TalkBack support. Tap to play as an alternative to dragging. Light and dark themes. Offline play with automatic sync when you reconnect.

FREE TO PLAY
No ads. No paywalls. No account needed to play. Create one only if you want to keep your streak across devices and join groups.

Download Icos and start your streak today.
