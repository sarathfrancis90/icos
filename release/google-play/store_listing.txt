APP TITLE (30 char max):
Icos - Daily Path Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 char max):
Draw a path, connect waypoints, fill every cell. One new puzzle every day.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char max):
Icos is a daily snake puzzle game that challenges you to draw a continuous path through a grid, connecting numbered waypoints in order while filling every cell. Simple rules, deep strategy.

HOW TO PLAY
Start at waypoint 1 and draw your path through the grid. Visit each numbered waypoint in sequence. Your goal: fill every cell with no gaps. Sounds easy? The grid has walls, tight turns, and tricky layouts that will make you think twice.

ONE PUZZLE PER DAY
Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle each day. Monday starts easy with a 5x5 grid. By Sunday, you're facing an 8x8 challenge. Compare your time with friends and see who solved it fastest.

FEATURES
- Daily puzzles that scale from easy to hard across the week
- Neon snake path with glowing animations and satisfying feedback
- Daily streaks with streak freezes to keep your run alive
- Groups and leaderboards to compete with friends and family
- Hints when you're stuck (3 per puzzle, use them wisely)
- Works offline with pre-cached puzzles
- Dark immersive gaming theme that looks stunning
- Share your results with friends

COMPETE WITH FRIENDS
Create groups, invite friends with a simple code, and compare solve times on daily and weekly leaderboards. Who finishes first? Who uses the fewest hints? The competition is friendly but fierce.

BUILD YOUR STREAK
Solve a puzzle every day to build your streak. Miss a day? Use a streak freeze to keep it alive. Track your longest streak and personal bests in your stats.

FREE TO PLAY
No ads. No paywalls. Just pure puzzle fun, one grid at a time.

Download Icos and start your streak today.
