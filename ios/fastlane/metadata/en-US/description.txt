Icos is a daily path puzzle. Draw one continuous line through every cell of the grid, visiting the numbered waypoints in order, without lifting your finger. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

THE ORIGINAL PATH PUZZLE
In 1857 the mathematician William Rowan Hamilton invented the Icosian game: find a route that visits every point exactly once. Icos brings that idea to your phone with a fresh grid every day.

ONE PUZZLE A DAY, FOR EVERYONE
The same puzzle for every player worldwide. Easy on Monday, expert by Sunday. Build a streak, earn a weekly streak freeze, and compare times with friends.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Create a group, share a six-letter invite code or QR, and race on daily and weekly leaderboards. A live activity feed shows when your friends solve.

PRACTICE AND ARCHIVE
Unlimited practice puzzles generated on your device, plus the last 30 days of dailies.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
Colorblind palettes with pattern overlays, full VoiceOver and TalkBack support, tap-to-play as an alternative to dragging, light and dark themes, and offline play with automatic sync.

No account needed to play. Create one to keep your streak across devices and join groups.
