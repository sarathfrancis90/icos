Icos is a daily path puzzle. Draw one continuous line through every cell of the grid, visiting the numbered waypoints in order. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

What's new in 1.1.0
- Icos moved to a new, faster backend. Progress starts fresh in this version.
- Sign in to an existing account straight from the guest cards (Google, Apple or email).
- Drawing over a cell you already filled no longer cuts your line.
- A fully light theme, a first-play guide and Tap to draw.
- Your streak updates correctly after a missed day and rolls over at midnight UTC while the app is open.
- Group admins can generate a new invite code; member counts refresh live, including when someone leaves.
- Archive puzzles up to 30 days old now count.
