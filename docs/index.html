How It Works

Simple to learn. Satisfying to master.

Every day brings a new grid puzzle. The rules are simple, but finding the path takes real thought.

1
①

Start at Waypoint 1

Tap the first waypoint and begin drawing your path through the grid. Swipe or tap adjacent cells to extend it.

2
🔌

Connect in Order

Visit each numbered waypoint in sequence. Plan ahead — you need to fill every cell while hitting every checkpoint.

3
🏆

Fill Every Cell

Complete the grid with no empty cells left. Beat the par time, share your result, and climb the leaderboard.
